## 2026-06-10 - String Concatenation and Iterator Overhead\n**Learning:** In Rust, avoid allocating a `Vec` of intermediate results just to join them into a string. Using `.collect::<String>()` directly on an iterator that yields types implementing `Display` or `AsRef<str>` is significantly more efficient. Additionally, avoid calling `.collect::<String>()` on single characters when `.to_string()` is sufficient.\n**Action:** Search for `.collect::<Vec<_>>().join("")` and replace it with `.collect::<String>()`. Replace `.collect::<String>()` on strings/chars with `.to_string()`.
